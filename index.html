Personal finance tracker

Just Give Me My Money

For Android. iPhone version planned.

See where your money is and where it went, across cash, bank accounts, cards and savings, in more than one currency. Everything stays on your phone: no account, no ads, no tracking.

What it does

  • Tracks income, spending and transfers across all your accounts.
  • Handles two currencies in one payment, including change handed back in another.
  • Records recurring payments by itself, or asks you to confirm them.
  • Keeps track of money you lent and money you owe.
  • Shows monthly reports by category.
