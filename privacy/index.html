Just Give Me My Money

Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026

The short version

  • Everything you enter stays on your own phone.
  • The app has no account, no servers, no ads and no tracking.
  • The developer cannot see your accounts, balances or transactions.

1. Who this policy is from

Just Give Me My Money (“the app”) is a personal finance tracker for Android and iOS, made by BigNeeme (“the developer”). This policy explains what happens to the information you put into the app.

2. What the app stores, and where

The app saves what you type into it so it can show your money back to you:

  • your accounts and their balances;
  • your transactions: amounts, dates, categories and any notes you add;
  • your recurring payments and reminder settings;
  • debts you record, including the name of the person you lent money to or borrowed from;
  • the categories and exchange rates you set, and your app settings.

All of this is kept in a private database on your phone. It is not sent to the developer or to anyone else.

3. What the developer collects

Nothing. The app does not collect, upload, share or sell any information. Specifically:

  • there is no sign-up and no account;
  • there are no analytics, advertising or tracking tools in the app;
  • the app does not read your contacts, location, photos, messages or bank accounts;
  • the app does not connect to your bank. Every figure in it is one you typed.

4. Your phone’s own backup

Android and iOS can back up app data to your Google or Apple account, or to your computer. If that backup is switched on, it may include this app’s data. The backup is made by your phone, is controlled by you in your phone’s settings, and is covered by Google’s or Apple’s own privacy policy. The developer has no access to it.

5. Permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications: to remind you when a recurring payment is due. Reminders are created on your phone; no message is sent from a server.
  • Alarms and reminders (Android): so those reminders arrive at the right time.

You can turn reminders off in the app’s Settings, or in your phone’s settings.

6. Deleting your data

You can erase everything at any time from Settings → Delete all data in the app. Uninstalling the app also removes its data from your phone. Because the developer holds none of your data, there is nothing to ask the developer to delete. If your phone’s backup kept a copy, you can remove it from your Google or Apple account.

7. Keeping your data safe

Your data sits in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot read, and is protected by your phone’s screen lock. The app does not currently have a lock of its own, so anyone who can use your unlocked phone can open it. Keep a screen lock on your phone.

8. Children

The app is made for a general audience and is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone, of any age.

9. The app stores

When you download the app, Google Play or the App Store handle the download and may collect their own information about it, under their own privacy policies. The developer receives only anonymous totals from the stores, such as the number of installs.

10. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds a feature that sends any information off your phone, such as live exchange rates or syncing between devices, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
